# Quollix Brand Policy

Normal contributions, private modifications, and forks made to submit changes upstream do not require permission or rebranding. You may keep the included assets in that context, provided you do not present your work as an official release or an independent product.

This policy helps people distinguish Quollix from independently released products and services, and explains how its brand assets may be used.

Newly added assets in these categories are also covered. Individual file notices are not required.

## Permitted Use

You may use the Quollix name and logo to truthfully refer to Quollix. Do not imply an official connection, endorsement, sponsorship, or certification that you do not have.

You may copy and display included Quollix branding when running or distributing an unmodified official release, including builds from its source. Normal configuration is allowed. Your build, distribution, or deployment does not become official by retaining the branding.

Photographs and portraits of the owner may remain included and displayed in their original context in these distributions. This does not permit their separate reuse or use to promote another product or service.

## Forks and Services

If you release a modified version or fork as an independent product, use your own primary branding. Preserve required copyright and license notices when rebranding.

You may offer hosting, support, consulting, or managed services for Quollix, including commercially, under your own branding. Unmodified software may retain its included branding, but that branding must not serve as the provider's own identity.

Do not adopt Quollix marks or confusingly similar marks as your own company, product, service, domain name, or logo. Accurate descriptive statements such as "based on Quollix," "hosting for Quollix," and "powered by Quollix" are allowed. They do not authorize logo use beyond the permissions above. Make clear who provides your product or service and do not imply endorsement by Quollix or the owner.
