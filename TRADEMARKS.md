# Quollix Trademarks and Branding

This policy covers Quollix branding, including the project name, logo, visual identity, and other brand assets.

## Permitted Use

You may use Quollix branding to truthfully refer to the project, for example to say that you use Quollix, provide compatibility with Quollix, run a service powered by Quollix, or offer hosting, consulting, support, or managed services for Quollix.

Commercial use of Quollix source code is allowed under the terms of its open source license.

If you run an unmodified Quollix instance for your own use or organization, including on your own server, you may keep the included branding.

You may state that your project is a fork of Quollix or is based on Quollix, provided that this is accurate and does not imply official status.

If you modify Quollix, fork it, or offer Quollix as a hosted or managed service, users must be able to clearly distinguish your offering from the official Quollix project. Use your own branding unless you have prior written permission.

## Prohibited Use

You may not use Quollix branding in a way that implies ownership, official affiliation, or endorsement when that is not true.

You do not acquire ownership of Quollix branding by running, hosting, modifying, or distributing Quollix.
