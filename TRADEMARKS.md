## General Rule

You may use Quollix branding to truthfully refer to Quollix. You may not use it in a way that suggests your project, product, service, or organization is official, endorsed, sponsored, certified, or operated by Quollix unless that is true.

## Unmodified Use

If you run or distribute an official, unmodified Quollix instance, source release, or build, you may keep the included branding.

## Modified Versions and Forks

If you modify Quollix, fork it, or distribute a modified version, use your own primary branding. You may state that your work is based on Quollix or forked from Quollix, provided that this is accurate.

## SaaS, Hosting, and Managed Services

You may offer hosting, support, consulting, or managed services for Quollix, including commercially. Use your own primary branding for your service, including its name, logo, and domain. You may truthfully describe the service as hosting Quollix, supporting Quollix, or being powered by Quollix.

If the Quollix software itself is unmodified, you may keep in-product attribution such as the included logo or "Powered by Quollix" notice.

Commercial use of Quollix source code is allowed under the MIT License. Commercial use of Quollix branding is allowed only as described in this policy or with prior written permission.
